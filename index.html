Agency Recommendation

Option 2 — Warm Sage (#7F9A84) · Jupiter Tile Match

Color-sampled from the Jupiter location's sea glass subway tile. Option 2 creates a direct bridge between the digital brand and the physical space — when someone sees Sea Glass on the website or social, it'll feel like the same palette they experience walking into the restaurant. It's sophisticated enough for the "lived-in luxury" positioning without tipping into the overly corporate territory the team flagged in the Feb 16 review. Warm, coastal, grounded.
